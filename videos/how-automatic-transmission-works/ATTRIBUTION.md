## Code-generated (no third-party asset file)

Everything on screen is drawn by code: the 3D world, the planetary gearset
(`Planetary3D`), the clutch pack (`Clutch3D`), the 3D bars, floating title icons,
kinetic text, and the animated stage. **Characters** are DiceBear (MIT) rendering
the **Open Peeps** style by Pablo Stanley (CC0), generated from a seed at render
time. No stock footage, no sourced images.

## Cited data sources (shown on a SourceCard)

- HowStuffWorks — How Automatic Transmissions Work (mechanism, gear ratios).
- fueleconomy.gov (U.S. DOE / EPA) — driving & gearing efficiency.

Illustrative gear ratios (≈ 3.1 / 1.8 / 1.4 / 1.0 / 0.71 / 4.5 : 1) are
representative of a typical 6-speed automatic, per the cited source.
